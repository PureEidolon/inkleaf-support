Inkleaf Notes Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. Inkleaf Notes is provided by Jianhe Cen.

Notes, images and recordings

The app stores your notebooks, handwriting, imported files and recordings on your device. Handwriting recognition and offline recording transcription run on your device. You can choose to export notes or share them through the iOS share sheet. The destination you choose handles those copies under its own privacy policy.

Language-model downloads and purchases

The app connects to model hosts to download recognition files. These requests do not upload your notes or recordings. Model hosts, including jsDelivr, Hugging Face and GitHub, handle network request data under their own policies. Apple processes in-app purchases and restores. The app uses StoreKit to determine your entitlements.

Permissions and storage

The camera, photo library and microphone are used when you choose to capture a document, import a picture or record audio. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings. Delete notebooks or recordings in the app to remove their local copies. Removing the app removes its local app data. Copies you previously exported are managed separately.

Support messages

If you contact support, your email address and any information you share are used to respond and resolve your request. The support and policy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages.

Questions or deletion requests for information you shared with support can be sent to cen082033@gmail.com.
