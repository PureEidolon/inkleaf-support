Inkleaf Notes Support

For help with Inkleaf Notes on iPhone and iPad, email cen082033@gmail.com. Include your device model and app version if relevant. Keep private notes and recordings out of the message unless you want to share them.

Writing with a finger

On a device without Apple Pencil, turn off the Pencil-only input option to write with a finger.

Purchases and trial

Open PRO next to Inkleaf on the home screen. Tap Restore Purchases using the Apple Account used for the original purchase. The optional seven-day trial does not renew automatically.

Offline recognition

Download a language model in the app settings before using that language offline. Recording requires microphone permission.
